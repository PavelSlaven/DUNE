# HD-remaster effects source prompts

The two reference sheets in this directory were generated with the built-in
image generation tool. They are project-bound source artwork for the local
D2TM remaster and are not intended as independently distributed artwork.

## `vfx_reference_4x3.png`

Inputs:

- `resources/hd/generated/ru4_preview1/effects_sheet.png`
- `resources/hd/generated/ru4_preview1/explosion_sheet.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high-resolution source sheet for a
> top-down 1990s Dune-inspired RTS remaster. Create exactly twelve isolated
> effects in a strict 4 by 3 grid: small orange bullet impact, medium orange
> bullet impact, bright cannon muzzle flash, small rocket exhaust; compact
> fire burst, large vehicle explosion, heavy structure explosion with debris,
> green gas explosion; soft gray-brown smoke plume, sand eruption, dark
> scorched crater, dusty vehicle track/debris burst. Use a perfectly flat black
> background. Preserve white/yellow/orange/red fire, brown-gray smoke and
> debris, acid-green gas and a dark brown-black crater. No text, labels, grid,
> borders, UI, objects, vehicles, characters or watermarks.

## `ground_reference_4x2.png`

Inputs:

- `artifacts/effects_classic_audit.png`
- `resources/hd/source_references/effects_remaster/vfx_reference_4x3.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high-resolution ground-effect and
> decal source sheet for a top-down Dune-inspired RTS remaster. Create exactly
> eight isolated top-down effects in a strict 4 by 2 grid. Row 1: small red
> infantry blood mark, medium red infantry blood mark, large red infantry
> blood mark, orange-brown scorched blast smudge. Row 2: sandworm mouth
> eruption in sand, fading sandworm trail wake, diagonal paired vehicle tread
> marks in sand, horizontal paired vehicle tread marks in sand. Use a perfectly
> flat black background. Preserve deep crimson blood, dark brown/orange
> scorching, ochre Arrakis sand and muted brown tracks. No text, labels, grid,
> borders, UI, objects, vehicles, characters or watermarks.
